# Print Layout

## Page Geometry

- Use A4 portrait by default.
- Use mirrored duplex margins: 18 mm inner, 12 mm outer, 14 mm top, and 15 mm bottom.
- Reserve the footer for page number and short source labels.
- Avoid a cover page for documents shorter than eight pages.

## Typography

- Use DengXian or another verified embedded Simplified Chinese font.
- Use 10.5 pt body text with approximately 1.45 line spacing.
- Use 16 pt title, 13 pt section heading, and 11 pt block heading.
- Keep formulas, diagrams, and screenshots large enough to remain readable after printing.

## Print Profiles

### Black And White

- Use black text, white backgrounds, and no more than four grayscale levels.
- Use borders, labels, and spacing rather than color alone to communicate hierarchy.
- Avoid fills darker than 15 percent except for small labels.
- Use line weights of at least 0.5 pt.

### Color

- Use restrained navy, teal, red, and neutral gray accents.
- Reserve red for warnings, mistakes, and high-priority review points.
- Ensure every colored distinction still works in grayscale.

## Shared Components

- Keep a question stem, its options, and essential diagram together when possible.
- Repeat headers on multi-page tables.
- Do not place long prose inside dense tables.
- Keep source labels small and gray, but readable.
- For answer space, use ruled lines rather than large empty boxes.
- PDF is the printing authority. DOCX must preserve content and structure but may paginate differently.
